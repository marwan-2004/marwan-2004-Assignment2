## Reverse String (Two Pointers Approach)
* **Explanation:** We initialize two pointers (`left` at index 0 and `right` at index `n - 1`). We swap their values in-place and move the pointers closer until they meet in the middle.
* **Time Complexity:** O(n) — We traverse half the array of size n.
* **Space Complexity:** O(1) — In-place swapping using a single temporary variable without allocating additional memory.
